## What was deliberately compressed

1. **Repeated navigation and introductory instructions:** one start page and a single progression replace repeated “next/previous” directions.
2. **Repeated case-page scaffolding:** the 13 analysis pages share a template—contract, timeline, dossier, what to copy/not copy, rewrite, transfer exercise. The course teaches that method once and selects relevant observations from cases.
3. **Repeated definitions:** ideas such as promise, open loop, proof, and beat are introduced in one lesson and then applied, rather than redefined on every page.
4. **Repeated examples:** one deeply analyzed scene and one opening comparison teach several connected decisions; extra versions remain optional.
5. **Reference versus instruction:** glossary entries and desk cards live in a reference file; learners do not have to study them as lessons.
6. **Template duplication:** forms with overlapping fields have been combined into ten tools.

No unique high-value skill has been removed for page-count alone. The compression removes repeated explanation, not the distinctions among real-video analysis, original scripts, and active workshop practice.
